Author of code: Vy, Adithi

The descriptive names for the fields and methods, like TryWalk, CanWalkTo, groundAhead, etc., make it easy to
understand their purpose without needing explanatory comments. UpdateGravity() exits early for 
dead enemies, so the main logic isn't nested. There are helpful comments like "Platforms support walking 
but do not act as side walls" to explain the why of the code. 

CanWalkTo() does a lot for one method. It computes the probe rectangles, filters non-solid block types,
checks wall collisions, ground support, and decides whether the enemy should turn or not. A change could 
be to split these responsibilities into a HitsWall() and IsSolidForEnemies() methods to help further
simplify the logic and make it easier to read.

Sprint: 4
File reviewed: MenuScreen.cs
Author of code: Vy, Jake

The Title and Instructions are read only properties with a clear and distinct job. Title has a default case that makes sense.
ActivateMain() is easy to read, and each case in the switch statement is short and concise.

The magic numbers in ActivateMain() only work as long as the menu items stay in that order.
If we wanted to reorder or insert an item, it will break the wiring. The hardcoded instructions
in Instructions will not update if a player rebinds the key bindings. 

Hypothetical change:
Remove the instructions from Instruction, most games don't have a tutorial that shows
players how to move with W/A/S/D keys in general. It's safe to assume players will know
what keys to use for movement and click the menu option to view the other key bindings that
aren't so obvious.
